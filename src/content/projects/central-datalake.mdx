## Context

Mortgage and lending data was distributed across silos, making it harder for teams to answer operational and strategic questions consistently. The central datalake created a shared foundation for proprietary business data and external market sources.

## A Shared Foundation for Lending and Market Data

The platform ingested mortgage and loan data alongside external market data into a governed AWS data layer. Stakeholder groups could then use the same foundation for self-service analytics, operational and strategic KPIs, machine-learning training, and product data access.

## Architecture

- Amazon S3 formed the central storage layer
- AWS Glue supported ingestion, processing, and cataloging
- Amazon Athena enabled serverless analytical queries
- AWS CDK made the infrastructure reproducible

## Outcome

The shared platform ended isolated data access patterns and shortened the path from a business question to a usable insight.

## Boundaries

The source systems, datasets, and stakeholder-specific metrics are proprietary. This account is limited to the platform pattern and organizational capabilities.
