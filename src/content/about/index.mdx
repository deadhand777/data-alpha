## How I Work

I treat AI as a product and an operating capability, not a model demonstration. A successful system needs reliable data, clear interfaces, evaluation, observability, governance, and a team that can operate it with confidence.

That is why I work across architecture, delivery, and organizational design. The goal is not simply to deploy new technology, but to make it useful, accountable, and maintainable.

## What You Will Find Here

This portfolio documents practical work in agentic AI, data platforms, machine learning, and regulated financial technology. The projects and articles focus on architecture, tradeoffs, and lessons that transfer beyond a single implementation.
