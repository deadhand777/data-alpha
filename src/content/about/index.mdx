## How I Work

I treat AI as a product and an operating capability, not a model demo. A system that works in production needs reliable data, clear interfaces, evaluation, observability, governance, and a team that can run it with confidence.

So I work across architecture, delivery, and how the organization is set up. Deploying the technology is the easy part. Making it useful, accountable, and maintainable is the job.

## What You Will Find Here

This portfolio documents practical work in agentic AI, data platforms, machine learning, and regulated financial technology. The projects and articles cover architecture, the tradeoffs behind it, and what transferred beyond the one implementation.
